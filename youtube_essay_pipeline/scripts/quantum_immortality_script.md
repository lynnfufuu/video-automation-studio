## 2. Core Premise & Hook
> According to the Many-Worlds interpretation, you can never truly die from your own perspective. Every time you face death, your conscious timeline splits, trapping you in an endless cascading reality where you are forced to survive increasingly agonizing, lonely nightmares.

**Scientific & Historical Foundation:** Hugh Everett's Many-Worlds Interpretation, Max Tegmark's Quantum Suicide thought experiment, and decoherence theory.
